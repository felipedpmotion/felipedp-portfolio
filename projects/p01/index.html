01 — Challenge

Tell 140 years of S-Class heritage — from the desert and the stars to the W140 blueprint — in one continuous show, built in weeks, for an ultra-wide LED wall split in two wings around a central arch.

02 — Approach

I wrote the detailed show script and storyboard, then generated every scene with AI (Nano Banana 2 for plates, AI video for motion) and composited the wall in After Effects: Arabic calligraphy forming in sand and starlight, a radio soundwave, the W140 blueprint, golden-light act transitions and a star of real light falling into the arch.

03 — Result

The show ran live at the regional launch on 29 Oct 2026, in front of the parade of classic S-Class generations.
