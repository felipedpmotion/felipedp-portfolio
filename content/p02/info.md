## Summary
A 46-second spot for Gulf Air at the Bahrain Grand Prix: a pilot and a driver, an airliner and an F1 car, racing as one on the same track.

## Challenge
Gulf Air wanted a story told through the circuit itself. The first step was getting the layout of the Bahrain International Circuit right, then turning it into a film where the airline and the race share the same tarmac.

## Approach
I designed the key scenes from the script as stills in Nano Banana Pro and Photoshop, animated them with Seedance 1.5 Pro and then Seedance 2.0 as soon as it was released, and recomposed and edited the film in After Effects and Premiere Pro.

## Result
Delivered as Gulf Air’s film for the Bahrain Grand Prix, closing on the line “Racing ahead as one”.
